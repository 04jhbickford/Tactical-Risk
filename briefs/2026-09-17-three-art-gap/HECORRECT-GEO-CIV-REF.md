## STEAL (geography only)
Civ/Polytopia-class **terrain readability**, not flat color blobs:
1. **Biome / climate washes** — snow/tundra → lush green → arid/tan, readable at mid zoom
2. **Mountain relief** — real height + soft directional shadow (not a brown stamp)
3. **Forest as massed vegetation** — clumps that read as woods, not a green fill
4. **Coast shelf** — light turquoise fringe vs deep ocean navy
5. **Rivers / inland water** — thin recessed blue drainage into sea
6. **Land undulation** — slight height variation so the board isn’t a flat sticker
